Commitments and measurement

KPIs fall into four groups and rise steadily month by month, with no inflated promises:

  • Rankings: keywords in the top 5 and top 10.
  • Organic traffic, estimated from the real search volume of the keyword set.
  • Presence in AI search (GEO).
  • Committed tasks each month.

Keywords already ranking at the top, such as your brand name, are tracked but not counted as commitments.

Frequently asked questions

How long until SEO shows results?

Usually 3 to 6 months before rankings and traffic rise clearly, depending on competition and the site's starting point. Technical and indexing issues can improve sooner.

Do you guarantee position one?

No. Nobody controls Google's algorithm. I commit to monthly numbers of keywords in the top 5 and top 10 and to specific tasks, all verifiable.

How is SEO priced?

By scope: number of pages, keywords and content volume. Get in touch for a detailed quote.
