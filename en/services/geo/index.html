What is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is optimising a website and brand information so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews understand the brand correctly, mention it and cite it in their answers.

Some call it AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or LLMO. The names differ; the goal is the same: become the source AI chooses to answer from.

Why does a business need GEO?

  • Customers use AI to compare options and find suppliers before visiting any website.
  • An AI answer usually names only a few brands and sources. Not being named means being ruled out from the start.
  • If information about you is inconsistent across the web, AI can describe your business wrongly: old services, old prices, old addresses.
  • Even when users don't click, being named by AI builds brand recognition.

How do AI tools choose sources to cite?

AI tools with web search usually answer in four steps. Each step is something to optimise:

  1. SearchThe AI runs queries against a search engine's index or its own crawler. Pages that aren't indexed, or that crawlers can't read, never enter the source list.
  2. Pick passagesThe system takes the most direct, clearest passages. Content split into short passages, one idea each, is picked more easily than a long, rambling article.
  3. Cross-checkInformation confirmed by several independent sources is more likely to be used. Brand information must match across your site and external sources.
  4. Compose the answerThe AI writes its answer and, depending on the platform, links to the sources it used.

This is how retrieval-augmented search systems generally work. Each platform differs, and none fully publishes how it selects sources.

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is optimising a website and brand information so AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews mention and cite the brand in their answers.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. AI tools usually draw on pages that are indexed and rank well, so solid SEO is the foundation for GEO.

How long until GEO shows results?

It depends on when each platform refreshes its data, typically a few weeks to a few months. Monthly reports on the same question set show the trend.

Do you guarantee my brand appears in ChatGPT?

Nobody can guarantee what an AI answers. I commit to tasks and measurable monthly indicators on an agreed question set.

Can I do GEO without SEO?

Yes, if the site already has a sound technical base and is well indexed. I check that before taking the project on.

What does GEO pricing depend on?

The number of tracked questions, platforms, pages to optimise and languages. Get in touch for a quote based on scope.
