What is a 1-on-1 SEO course?

A 1-on-1 SEO course is one where a single tutor works with a single learner, using the learner's own website as the course material. Instead of attending a general lecture and working out how to apply it alone, you learn to read data, diagnose problems and make decisions on the site you're actually responsible for.

The course focuses on "why": why a page isn't indexed, why two pages compete, why merging beats writing something new. Once you understand the reasons, you can handle situations the course never covered.

What a session looks like

  1. Before the sessionYou send your questions and grant view access to the data needed (Search Console, GA4). I prepare examples from your site.
  2. First ~70 minutesAn in-depth walkthrough of the session's topic, every example from your site.
  3. Next ~40 minutesWorking together on real files and tools: Search Console, Screaming Frog, spreadsheets.
  4. Final 10 minutesAgree the actions and the exercise to apply to your site before the next session.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the 1-on-1 SEO course?

The standard outline is 10 sessions, two a week, two hours each, so about five weeks.

What do I need to know beforehand?

You should have a website you work on and know basic SEO concepts. The course suits people with 6 months to a year of SEO experience best.

Can I take it without my own website?

You need a site you're allowed to analyse, such as your employer's. Without one, the course loses most of its value.

Online or in person?

Online, 1-on-1. Sessions are scheduled around your time.

Does the course guarantee rankings?

No. It teaches you to understand and do SEO properly. Rankings depend on what you implement afterwards and how competitive your industry is.
