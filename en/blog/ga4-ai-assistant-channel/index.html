GA4 adds an AI Assistant channel: measuring traffic from ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, plus GA4's 2026 updates

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 2 min read.

On 13 May 2026, Google Analytics 4 added an "AI Assistant" default channel. Visits from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude are now recognised automatically and separated into their own channel instead of being mixed into Referral. It's the simplest way for a business to see how many users AI is sending to its site.

1. What is the AI Assistant channel in GA4?

AI Assistant is a channel in GA4's Default channel group containing visits whose referrer is a recognised AI assistant. When someone clicks a link in a ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude answer and lands on your site, GA4 records it as follows:

FieldValue
Default channel groupAI Assistant
Mediumai-assistant
Campaign(ai-assistant)

Google gives ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude as examples but hasn't published a full list of recognised sources.

2. Before and after the AI Assistant channel

Before May 2026From May 2026
ChatGPT trafficIn Referral, filtered by source manuallyAutomatically in AI Assistant
Quick reportingRequired a custom channel groupBuilt into the Acquisition reports
Comparison with Organic SearchHard, data scatteredSide by side in the same report

On 11 June 2026, GA4 also added Source Group, with built-in grouping for newer sources such as ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Perplexity.

3. Limits to keep in mind

  • Only clicks are counted. GA4 can't see mentions nobody clicked.
  • It depends on referrer data. If an AI app doesn't pass a referrer, the visit may land in Direct.
  • AI Overviews and AI Mode aren't in this channel. Their clicks go through Google Search and still count as Organic Search.

So GA4 is just one of several sources for measuring GEO.

Frequently asked questions

When did GA4 add the AI Assistant channel?

Google Analytics added the AI Assistant channel to the default channel group on 13 May 2026.

Does traffic from Google AI Overviews go into AI Assistant?

No. Clicks from AI Overviews and AI Mode go through Google Search and still count as Organic Search.

Why is ChatGPT traffic in GA4 lower than expected?

GA4 only counts people who click through. Many users stop at the answer, and some AI apps don't pass referrer data, so visits may land in Direct.
