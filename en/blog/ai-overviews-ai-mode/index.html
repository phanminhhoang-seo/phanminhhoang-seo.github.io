What are AI Overviews and AI Mode? How they differ from regular results

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 1 min read.

AI Overviews are AI-written summaries shown right above Google's search results. AI Mode is a separate mode where people ask follow-up questions, as with a chatbot. Both are available in Vietnam in Vietnamese, and both change how customers reach websites.

2. Where do AI Overviews and AI Mode get their information?

According to Google's documentation:

  • Both may use query fan-out: running several related searches across subtopics and combining the results into one answer.
  • So the links shown can be more varied than the ten results of a single regular search.
  • To be linked, a page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet in Google Search. Google says there are no extra technical requirements for AI features.

3. What this means for websites

  • Fewer clicks on simple questions: users may get what they need from the answer without clicking.
  • A chance for pages outside the top spots: thanks to query fan-out, a page that answers a subtopic well can still be linked.
  • Data is only starting to be separated: since June 2026 Google has been testing generative AI performance reports in Search Console for some sites, with impressions but no clicks yet. See how measuring GEO differs from SEO.

Frequently asked questions

Are AI Overviews available in Vietnam?

Yes. AI Overviews started appearing in Vietnam, with Vietnamese support, in late October 2024.

How is AI Mode different from AI Overviews?

AI Overviews are a summary shown above regular results. AI Mode is a separate mode where users ask and follow up, like chatting with a chatbot.

Can I see AI Overviews traffic in Search Console?

The data is counted in the main Performance report. Since June 2026 Google has been testing separate generative AI performance reports for some sites, currently with impressions but no clicks.
