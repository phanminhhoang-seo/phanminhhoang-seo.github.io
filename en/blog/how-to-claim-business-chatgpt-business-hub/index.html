How to claim your business in ChatGPT Business Hub: step-by-step verification

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 4 min read.

To claim your business in ChatGPT Business Hub, open OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT page, click Claim your business, sign in, find or add your business, verify ownership (by phone, email, website domain or Shopify), then complete an identity check for the profile manager. This guide walks through each step with real screenshots, plus the cases where you can't claim yet.

1. Before you start

ChatGPT Business Hub is where businesses claim and manage their profile in ChatGPT, part of the Businesses in ChatGPT programme OpenAI launched in beta on 10 November 2026. What Business Hub contains and what its data shows is covered in ChatGPT Business Hub shows businesses their mentions and clicks. This guide focuses only on signing up.

ItemDetails
CostFree at launch

Who can claimEligible businesses in markets where ChatGPT operates and OpenAI supports verification

Business AgentEligible US ecommerce and online service businesses with a website

Note: Business Hub is different from the paid ChatGPT Business plan for teams. The names overlap, but they're unrelated.

2. Steps to claim your business

Step 1: Open the Businesses in ChatGPT page

Go to OpenAI's official page at openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt and click Claim your business. The page sums up the purpose: claim your business to share what you offer, keep your information up to date, and understand how you show up.

Step 2: Sign in and search for your business

Sign in with your OpenAI account. The next screen has a Search business box for finding a profile ChatGPT already has. If yours isn't there, continue to add it.

Step 3: Enter your business website

Enter your official website, for example https://yourbusiness.com/. It's used for verification and for counting mentions and clicks in Business Hub.

Step 4: Verify ownership

According to reports, OpenAI verifies businesses through one of the following, depending on the business:

MethodSuits
Business phone numberBusinesses with a public phone number
Business emailAn email on the same domain as the website
Domain DNS recordBusinesses that control their domain's DNS
Shopify accountStores running on Shopify

For DNS verification, OpenAI gives you a TXT record to add in your domain settings:

  • Type: TXT
  • Name/Host: openai-business-profile
  • Value: your own string, starting with openai-business-profile=

Once the record is added, return to Business Hub to confirm. DNS changes can take a few minutes to a few hours to take effect.

Step 5: Verify the manager's identity

After the business is verified, the person managing the profile must pass an identity check through Persona, the identity verification provider OpenAI uses, before editing the profile or creating a Business Agent.

Step 6: Update your profile and track data

  • Keep official details current: description, services, products, contact information.
  • Track mentions, website clicks and common topics. Data is aggregated over 28 days and only appears once minimum thresholds are met.
  • If eligible, create a Business Agent to answer customers inside ChatGPT.

The first case is what happened when I tried to claim this very site: a github.io address doesn't allow adding a TXT record, so DNS verification couldn't go any further.

4. Will claiming get you mentioned more?

According to OpenAI, ChatGPT keeps making independent recommendations, and claiming a profile doesn't guarantee better placement or more sales. Business Hub is valuable for two things:

  • Official information: less risk of ChatGPT describing your business wrongly, as discussed in why AI gets your brand wrong.
  • Data from ChatGPT itself: a new source for measuring GEO, alongside Search Console and GA4.

Business Hub only covers ChatGPT. Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity get their information differently, as explained in what is GEO.

Frequently asked questions

Does ChatGPT Business Hub cost anything?

No. At launch, claiming a business profile in Business Hub is free and doesn't need a ChatGPT Business plan or ads account.

How can a business be verified?

Depending on the business, OpenAI verifies through a phone number, business email, domain DNS record or Shopify account. The manager then completes an identity check.

Can a github.io site be claimed?

It can't be verified via DNS, because you don't control the github.io domain's DNS. Use another method if available, or move to your own domain.

Will claiming make ChatGPT recommend me more?

OpenAI says claiming doesn't guarantee better placement. The main benefits are official information and data on mentions and clicks.
