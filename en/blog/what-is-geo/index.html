What is GEO? Generative Engine Optimization explained simply

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 3 min read.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is getting AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to understand your brand correctly, name it and cite it in their answers. If SEO is about appearing in the list of results, GEO is about appearing inside the answer itself.

1. What is GEO?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization. "Generative engines" are systems that write an answer instead of only returning a list of links: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, and AI Overviews and AI Mode in Google Search.

In short, GEO is about three questions:

  • When customers ask AI about your field, is your brand named?
  • Does the answer link to your website?
  • Does the AI describe you correctly: services, prices, location, contact details?

2. GEO, AEO, LLMO: one goal, several names

The field is new and the terms aren't settled. You'll see these names, which mostly mean the same thing:

NameFull formEmphasis
GEOGenerative Engine OptimizationGenerative engines in general

In Vietnam, AI Overviews started showing in Vietnamese in late October 2024, and AI Mode added Vietnamese in October 2025. AI Overviews and AI Mode compared with regular results covers both in more detail.

4. How is GEO different from SEO?

SEOGEO
GoalRankings and clicks on the results pageBeing named and cited in the answer
What users seeTen blue linksOne answer synthesised from several sources
MeasurementSearch Console, rankings, trafficTracked questions, citation count, AI referral traffic

One doesn't replace the other. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode only link to pages that are indexed and eligible to show a snippet, so SEO remains the foundation. The biggest difference is measurement, covered in how measuring GEO differs from SEO.

5. Why should businesses care now?

  • Habits are changing: many people ask AI to compare and shortlist suppliers before opening any website.
  • Fewer slots: an AI answer usually names only a few brands. Not being named means not being on the shortlist.
  • Risk of being described wrongly: AI can get your business wrong, as explained in why AI gets your brand wrong.
  • Platforms are starting to share data: OpenAI just launched ChatGPT Business Hub, showing businesses their mentions and clicks.

Frequently asked questions

What does GEO stand for?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization: optimising for generative search tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. AI tools usually draw on pages that are indexed and rank well. SEO is the foundation; GEO extends the goal to being named and cited in answers.

Is GEO different from AEO?

The goal is almost the same. AEO emphasises becoming the answer, GEO emphasises generative engines. Many people use the two interchangeably.
