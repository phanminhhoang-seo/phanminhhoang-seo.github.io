ChatGPT Business Hub: OpenAI shows businesses their mentions and clicks

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 2 min read.

On 10 November 2026, OpenAI launched the Businesses in ChatGPT programme in beta. With it comes Business Hub, where verified businesses can see how often ChatGPT points to their website, how many clicks it sends and which topics come up. It's the first time a major AI platform has shared data like this, which is why many are calling it "Search Console for ChatGPT".

1. What's in Business Hub?

  • Mentions: the number of ChatGPT answers that reference the business's website. According to OpenAI's definitions as reported, naming the brand without referencing the site doesn't count.
  • Website clicks: recorded clicks on links to the website from ChatGPT. OpenAI notes these don't confirm visits or conversions.
  • Discovery topics: common themes in conversations where the business was mentioned.
  • Business information: owners keep details and products up to date. Eligible businesses can also publish a Business Agent that answers customers inside ChatGPT.

2. What it doesn't have yet

  • No individual user prompts.
  • No competitor comparison.
  • ChatGPT data only; no other AI platforms.
  • According to early analyses, data covers the last 28 days.

OpenAI also states that claiming a business doesn't guarantee more visibility in answers.

5. What it means for businesses

  • For the first time, there's first-party data from an AI platform, not just third-party estimates.
  • It shows GEO measurement is maturing, much as Search Console did for SEO.
  • But the data is partial: one platform, no prompts, no competitors. A full picture still needs several sources, as discussed in how measuring GEO differs from SEO.

While Business Hub rolls out, you can still find out how your brand shows up across ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews.

Frequently asked questions

What is ChatGPT Business Hub?

It's the dashboard in OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT programme, launched in beta on 10 November 2026. Verified businesses can see mentions, website clicks and common topics from ChatGPT.

Can businesses outside the US use it?

Reports say the initial rollout favours ecommerce and online service businesses in the US. There's no official timeline for other countries yet.

Does Business Hub replace GEO tracking tools?

Not yet. It only covers ChatGPT, shows no individual prompts and has no competitor comparison, so other sources are still needed.
