Search Console gets generative AI performance reports: AI Overviews and AI Mode separated for the first time

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 1 min read.

On 3 June 2026, Google announced Search Generative AI performance reports in Search Console. For the first time, site owners can see separately which pages appear in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover's AI features. The reports are rolling out to a test group of sites and don't include clicks yet.

1. What are the generative AI performance reports?

The generative AI performance reports are a new view in Google Search Console that separates AI feature data from the main Performance report. Previously, impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode were counted but merged with regular results, so you couldn't tell which part came from AI.

Note: AI data still counts in the totals of the main Performance report. The new reports simply break that part out.

2. What the reports show

DataMeaningScope
ImpressionsHow often the site's URLs appeared in AI featuresSearch and Discover
PagesWhich URLs appearedSearch and Discover
CountriesImpressions by countrySearch and Discover
DevicesDesktop, mobileSearch only
DatesHourly, daily, weekly, monthlySearch and Discover

3. What's missing

  • No clicks or CTR in the data Google listed.
  • Unclear how position is reported inside AI answers.
  • Unclear how far back the data goes.
  • Not available to every site: Google is rolling out to "a subset of websites" for testing, with no criteria or wider timeline announced.

Google says it may add metrics over time but hasn't named any.

4. What it means for GEO measurement

  • Google AI Overviews and AI Mode finally have first-party data, much as ChatGPT has Business Hub and Bing has its AI Performance report.
  • You can see which pages AI uses: page-level data shows what Google picks for its answers.
  • Not yet enough for business impact: without clicks, AI impressions can't be tied to traffic and conversions.

Frequently asked questions

When did Search Console launch generative AI performance reports?

Google announced them on 3 June 2026 on the Google Search Central Blog. They are rolling out to a subset of sites for testing.

Do the reports include clicks from AI Overviews?

Not yet. Google listed impressions, pages, countries, devices and dates. Clicks and CTR are not included.

What if my site doesn't have the report?

Google has opened it to a test group only and hasn't announced a wider rollout. AI data is still counted in the main Performance report, just not separated.
