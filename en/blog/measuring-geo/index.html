How is measuring GEO different from measuring SEO?

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 2 min read.

SEO has one shared data source, Google Search Console, with rankings, impressions and clicks. GEO has nothing like it yet: data is spread across platforms, AI answers change from one ask to the next, and many mentions produce no click at all. This article compares the two so you can set the right expectations.

1. At a glance

SEOGEO
Unit of measurementKeywords and URLsQuestions (prompts) and answers
Main metricsRankings, impressions, clicks, CTRMention rate, citation count, AI traffic
Data sourcesSearch Console, rank trackersMany sources, each platform different
StabilityRankings fairly stable within a dayAnswers change with every ask
Query dataReal query listsAlmost no real user questions

2. Common GEO metrics

  • Mention rate: out of the tracked question set, how many answers name the brand.
  • Citation count: how many answers link to the site, and which pages are used as sources.
  • Accuracy of description: whether AI gets services, prices and location right.
  • Position against competitors: named first or last, and alongside whom.
  • AI referral traffic: visits from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and gemini.google.com, measured in GA4.

4. Why GEO numbers are harder to pin down

  • Answers vary: ask the same question twice and you may get two different brand lists.
  • Personalisation: chat history, location and mode (with or without web search) all change the answer.
  • No query data: platforms rarely reveal what users actually asked.
  • Mentions without clicks: many people stop after reading the answer, so traffic understates presence.

5. What this means for setting KPIs

  • Report trends month by month rather than a single measurement.
  • Measure each question several times to smooth out answer variation.
  • Combine platform data (Search Console, Bing, Business Hub) with your own tracked questions.
  • Be clear with stakeholders that these are correlations; attribution isn't as precise as in SEO.

Frequently asked questions

Can Search Console measure GEO?

Partly. Impressions and clicks from AI Overviews and AI Mode are counted in the Performance report, but merged into the Web search type, not separated. Search Console has no data from ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude.

What is the most important GEO metric?

The share of questions customers actually ask in your industry where your brand is named, measured repeatedly each month. AI traffic is a secondary metric because many mentions produce no click.

Why does AI give a different answer each time?

Language model output has a random element and also depends on chat history, location and whether web search is used. That's why you measure several times and look at the trend.
