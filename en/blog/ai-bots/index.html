Types of AI bots: how GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot differ

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 2 min read.

There isn't one kind of AI bot. Each AI company usually runs several bots for different jobs: some collect data to train models, some index pages for search features, and some only visit a page when a user asks. Knowing the groups tells you which bots matter for your brand being cited in answers.

3. Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended aren't bots

These two names are often mistaken for separate crawlers:

  • Google-Extended has no user agent of its own. It's a robots.txt token that controls whether content Google crawls may be used to train Gemini and for Gemini grounding. Google says it doesn't affect inclusion in Google Search.
  • Applebot-Extended is similar: it controls whether data Applebot collects may be used to train Apple's models.

4. Which bots matter for being mentioned?

  • Search bots (OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot) decide whether your pages enter the pool of sources AI can cite.
  • User-triggered bots read a page the moment they're asked. If it's slow or broken, the content isn't used.
  • Training bots act more slowly: they shape what a model already knows about your brand, even without web search.
  • Most AI bots don't run JavaScript. Content that only appears after JavaScript runs is close to invisible to them, as covered in indexing cleanup.

Is your site allowing, or accidentally blocking, the AI bots that matter? It's one of the first things I check in a GEO baseline.

Frequently asked questions

If I block GPTBot, will my site disappear from ChatGPT?

According to OpenAI, GPTBot is for training while OAI-SearchBot powers ChatGPT's search features. They are separate bots and are configured separately in robots.txt.

Is Google-Extended a Google crawler?

No. Google-Extended has no user agent; it's a robots.txt token controlling whether content is used for Gemini. Google says it doesn't affect Google Search.

Can AI bots read JavaScript content?

According to Vercel's analysis (December 2024), crawlers from OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity and Meta don't execute JavaScript. Gemini, using Google's infrastructure, and Applebot do.
