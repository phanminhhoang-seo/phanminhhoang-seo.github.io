Ads in AI Mode expand to 10 more countries: what businesses need to know

Phan Minh Hoàng

SEO and GEO specialist, SEO Leader at Forza

Published 13/11/2026. 2 min read.

On 28 October 2026, Google expanded ads in AI Mode to 10 English-speaking markets outside the US, including Malaysia, Singapore and the Philippines. On 9 November 2026, Google added ads on follow-up questions in a conversation (multiturn), starting in the US. Vietnam isn't on the list, but it's a direction search marketers should watch.

2. Which campaigns are eligible?

  • Existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible, with no separate setup or budget.
  • Broad match, AI Max for Search, Shopping and Performance Max match on both the query and the conversation context.
  • Exact and phrase match only show when the query has clear commercial intent.
  • Google is also testing local inventory and vehicle ads.

3. Ads on follow-up questions (multiturn)

Multiturn ads are ads shown on follow-up questions within an AI Mode conversation. They can match the question directly or use context from earlier questions and answers, so they can appear even before a user searches for a specific product. They're launching in the US, with no timeline for other markets.

5. What it means for businesses in Vietnam

  • Organic results and ads in AI Mode are growing side by side. As ads take more space in answers, being cited organically becomes more valuable.
  • Businesses selling into English-speaking markets such as Singapore, Malaysia or Australia should review Performance Max and broad match campaigns, which run in AI Mode automatically.
  • The lack of separate reporting makes AI Mode performance hard to judge, much like organic search before the AI reports in Search Console.

Frequently asked questions

Are ads in AI Mode available in Vietnam?

Not yet. As of November 2026, ads in AI Mode run in the US and 10 English-speaking markets, in English only. Google hasn't announced Vietnam.

Can I opt out of ads in AI Mode?

Not separately. AI Mode and AI Overviews can't be split from Search; advertisers can only use negative keywords, asset pinning and brand exclusions.

What are multiturn ads?

Ads shown on follow-up questions in an AI Mode conversation, based on the question and earlier context. They launched in the US in November 2026.
