What is W320 cashew?

In the AFI grading system, “W” stands for white whole kernels and “320” is the number of kernels per pound: a W320 pound holds 300 to 320 kernels. It sits in the middle of the whole-kernel range, larger than W450 and smaller than W240 or W180.

That balance of size and price is why W320 is the most traded cashew grade worldwide and the default choice for roasters and snack brands in the US.

W320 compared with other whole grades

W180 / W210Largest kernels, premium gift and retail packs
W240Large kernels for premium roasted and salted lines
W320Standard size for snacks, roasting and mixed nuts
W450Smaller kernels for value packs and ingredients

Applications

  • Roasting and seasoning: roasted and salted, honey roasted, flavored cashews.
  • Snack and trail mixes: consistent size blends well with almonds and dried fruit.
  • Retail repacking: supermarket and club-store bags under your own label.
  • Food manufacturing: bakery, confectionery and plant-based products. For butter and pastes, splits and pieces are the lower-cost option.

Quality control from raw nut to container

Every lot is processed at our own factory in Binh Phuoc, Vietnam's main cashew region, with more than 15,000 m² of production space.

  1. 1. Raw nut intakeRaw cashew nuts are checked for moisture and out-turn before processing.
  2. 2. Shelling and peelingKernels are separated from the shell and testa is removed.
  3. 3. GradingKernels are sorted by size and color to the AFI standard, with color sorters.
  4. 4. Foreign-matter controlX-ray inspection before packing.
  5. 5. PackingVacuum packed in 50 lb tins or bag-in-carton.
  6. 6. Pre-shipment checkFinal check before loading; test report available on request.

Shipping to the US

We ship FOB Ho Chi Minh City or CFR/CIF to main US ports such as Los Angeles / Long Beach, Oakland, New York / New Jersey and Savannah. A 20ft container holds about 700 cartons (15.9 MT) of W320.

Each shipment comes with the documents US importers need for customs and FDA review:

  • Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading
  • Certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate
  • Certificate of analysis for the lot
  • Supplier documents for your FSVP file on request

W240 kernels are larger (220–240 per pound) and priced higher. W320 is smaller, more widely traded and the usual choice for roasting and snack mixes.
